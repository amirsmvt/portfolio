About Me

AmirHossein Samavat

|

I'm a Unity / C# game developer with several years of experience building mobile games, and I hold a Master's degree in Artificial Intelligence, which I completed with Distinction. I've worked across genres, including match-3, puzzle, card, idle, multiplayer and action games, and my work has shipped on both Android and iOS.

At studios such as Medrick, Baxter and Epicade, I've built LiveOps events, online multiplayer systems with Photon and FishNet, and iOS ports. I've also optimised game performance and led a development team as Scrum Master. I'm especially interested in AI and cognitive games: both of my degree projects were cognitive games, including research tools for assessing children's memory and attention.

I currently work at a game studio and take on freelance projects. I work well on my own and in teams, and I'm always happy to talk about new projects and opportunities. Feel free to reach out!
